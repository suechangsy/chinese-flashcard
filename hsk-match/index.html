🔊 Install a Chinese Voice · Official Guides

Choose your device below. These links explain how to download a voice in your device settings. An internet connection is needed for the download. Choose Mandarin / Chinese (Simplified, China) when available.

  • Windows 10 / 11

    Open Settings → Time & Language → Language options (or Language & region). Add Chinese (Simplified, China), then install its Text-to-speech feature. You do not need to change your display language.

    Microsoft: Download languages and voices ↗
  • Mac

    Open System Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (called Spoken Content in some versions). Open System voice and its voice options, choose a Mandarin Chinese voice, and download it.

    Apple: Download voices on Mac ↗
  • iPhone / iPad

    Open Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (or Spoken Content) → Voices. Choose Chinese / Mandarin and download a voice if offered.

    Apple: Speech and voice settings ↗
  • Android

    Search Settings for Text-to-speech output. Open your speech engine settings → Install voice data, then choose Chinese / Mandarin. Menu names vary by device.

    Google: Install voice data ↗

After installing, restart your browser and reopen this game. Some system voices are not available to every browser. This game uses only offline Chinese voices made available by your browser.

Lesson progress and review banks are saved by level. Total points are saved across all levels on this device and browser. Clearing browser data or switching browsers/devices will not keep these records.

About the lesson vocabulary

This game follows the six-level HSK Standard Course (HSK 2.0 textbook series), including Books A and B for levels 4–6. It practices characters drawn from lesson vocabulary, not every character in the texts or exercises. Level 1 retains the original word list.

Level 2–5 vocabulary transcriptions: HSK Standard Course Vocabulary. Level 6 core vocabulary: HSK Standard Course Anki, checked against lesson vocabulary tables. Pronunciations are prepared offline using source transcriptions and the pypinyin dictionary. Compare the Lesson Word List with your textbook edition.
